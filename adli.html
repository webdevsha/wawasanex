Adli Amirullah

Non-Executive Director and Chief Economist

Adli Amirullah is an economist by training with more than 10 years of experience across economic research, public policy, external affairs strategy and government relations. His areas of expertise include the digital economy, business regulation, technology policy and SME development.

Over the course of his career, Adli has authored more than 10 different independent studies and contributed to policy discussions across digital economy & platform regulation, business regulation & market governance, and transport & mobility policy. His experience across think tanks, government agencies and the private sector has given him a multidisciplinary perspective on how economic policy, regulation and business strategy intersect.

At Wawasanex, Adli serves as Non-Executive Director and Chief Economist, contributing to the organisation’s economic research, policy analysis and public discourse on issues affecting Malaysia’s development. He is particularly interested in translating economic evidence into practical policy recommendations and strengthening collaboration between policymakers, industry and civil society.

Adli holds a Bachelor of Economics from the International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM), where he received the Royal Education Award (Anugerah Pelajaran Diraja), and a Master of Research (MRes) in Economics from the University of Malaya, where he was awarded the King’s Scholarship (Biasiswa Yang di-Pertuan Agong).
